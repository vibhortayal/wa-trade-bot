How to read this page

Every hour, a bot reads the trading group chat and pulls out the actual trades people mention — buys, sells, options, plans. Everyone is anonymous (Trader 01, Trader 02…): no names, no message text, just the trade itself.

Trade tape
Every action mentioned, in time order. The colored word is what they did: BUY opened · ADD added to one · SELL sold · TRIM sold part of it · EXIT closed it completely · PLAN “I'll buy if…” · HOLD / WATCH sitting tight.
Tap a card
Tap anything for a popup with its numbers — actions, the ✓/✗/– record, hit rate, average move. Where a filter fits, the popup offers a "Filter tape" button. Tapping never changes the page on its own.
Outcome badge
How the trade did over the next 5 trading days: ✓ moved in the trade's favor · ✗ moved against · – stayed flat (within 1%). For options it scores the stock's direction, not the contract's profit. Tap any badge for details.
The dot
How sure the bot is it read the message correctly — sure · maybe · shaky.
