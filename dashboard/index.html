How to read this page

Every hour, a bot reads the trading group chat and pulls out the actual trades people mention — buys, sells, options, plans. Everyone is anonymous (Trader 01, Trader 02…): no names, no message text, just the trade itself.

Trade tape
Every action mentioned, in time order. The colored word is what they did: BUY opened a new position · ADD added to one · SELL sold · TRIM sold part of it · EXIT closed it completely · PLAN “I'll buy if…” · HOLD / WATCH sitting tight.
The dot
How sure the bot is it read the message correctly — sure · maybe · shaky.
Outcome badge
How the trade did over the next 5 trading days: ✓ +3.2% moved in the trade's favor · ✗ −1.5% moved against · – 0.4% stayed flat (within 1%). For options the badge scores the stock's direction, not the contract's profit — option prices aren't in the chat. Plans show ✓ target hit when the price touched the announced target within 14 days. Tap any badge for details.
Net lean
Opening trades minus closing trades. +3 accumulating = three more opens than closes — money going in.
Put / call
Bearish option bets ÷ bullish ones. Above 1 = the group is betting more on downside than upside.
Crowd clusters
Symbols that 3 or more members all traded — where the group agrees.
Options lens
How far out the options bets expire: this month, 1–6 months, or LEAPS (6+ months).
Plans watchlist
Conditional orders people announced, e.g. “buy NVDA if it breaks 180”.

Tip: tap any symbol in the leaderboard to filter the whole page to just that symbol.
